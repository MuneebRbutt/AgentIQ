# Participant information and consent

**Study:** Usability of AgentIQ, an AI assistant that answers calls, messages and emails for
small businesses. Final year project, [university and department].
**Researchers:** [team names and contact email]. **Supervisor:** [name and email].

## What you will do

You will use the AgentIQ dashboard to set up a pretend business and complete six short tasks,
thinking aloud as you go. You may also call or text a demo phone number. Afterwards you will fill
in a ten-question form and answer a few questions about your experience. It takes about
45 minutes.

## What we record

- Whether you completed each task, how long it took, and notes of what you said.
- Your answers to the questionnaire and interview.
- **Only if you tick the box below:** a recording of the screen (not your face).

Everything is stored under a participant code, not your name. Only the project team and
supervisor see it, and it is deleted when the project has been marked. Results appear in the
report only as totals and anonymous quotes.

## Please do not share real personal information

The tasks use made-up businesses and customers. Please use only the details on the task cards.
**Do not give real health information, real card numbers or anyone's real details**, even
when the task is about a clinic.

## Your rights

Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without giving a reason, and ask us to delete
your data up to [date], by emailing [contact email]. There is no payment, and no risk beyond
ordinary computer use.
