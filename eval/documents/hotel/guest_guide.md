# Grand Lahore Hotel: guest guide

Synthetic evaluation document (AgentIQ M10). Not a real hotel.

## Early check-in and late check-out

Early check-in from 10:00 AM can be arranged for PKR 3,000, subject to availability.
Late check-out until 3:00 PM costs PKR 2,500. Please ask the front desk a day ahead.

## Laundry

Laundry handed in by 9:00 AM is returned the same evening. Washing and ironing costs
PKR 300 per shirt and PKR 400 per pair of trousers.

## Gym

The gym is on the third floor and is open from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM every day.

## Wi-Fi

Free high-speed Wi-Fi is available in all rooms and public areas. The password is printed
on your key card holder.

## Airport shuttle

The shuttle must be booked at least 6 hours before your flight or arrival. The drive to
Allama Iqbal International Airport takes about 30 minutes.
