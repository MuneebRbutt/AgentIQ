# CareWell Family Clinic: patient guide

Synthetic evaluation document (AgentIQ M10). No real patients or doctors.

## Childhood vaccinations

Routine childhood vaccines are given on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM.
Please bring the child's vaccination card.

## Blood tests

Blood samples are collected Monday to Saturday from 9:00 AM to 11:00 AM. Fasting tests
need 10 hours without food. Most results are ready within 24 hours.

## ECG

An ECG costs PKR 1,500 and takes about 15 minutes. No appointment is needed during
opening hours.

## Parking

Free parking is available behind the clinic building, entrance from Zafar Ali Road.

## Medical certificates

Sick-leave certificates are issued only after a consultation.
